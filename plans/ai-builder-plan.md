## The offer (prices are proposals to test)

1. **AI build** (one-time, about $1,500-3,000 to start). A no-code system that removes one repetitive job. Examples:
   - AI-drafted guest and customer replies in the owner's voice, approved with one tap
   - Lead and booking follow-up that runs on its own
   - Intake or quote form that feeds a CRM or spreadsheet, plus an automatic reply
   - Tools: Zapier or Make, ChatGPT or Claude, ManyChat, the business's own booking and email apps
2. **Care plan** (monthly, about $150-300). Fixes, tune-ups, small changes, and a monthly check-in.

Sell the result ("guests get a reply in minutes, you get your evenings back"), never "I'll set up AI for you."
Never promise bookings or sales numbers.

## Week 1: Validate before building anything

Goal: find out whether owners will pay before you spend months on this.

**Talk to 5-10 local owners.** Listen only, no pitch. Good candidates:
- Lodging and cabin-rental owners, outfitters, rental shops (you already know this niche)
- Contractors and home-service businesses (lots of quote requests)
- Small shops, salons, clinics, gyms that live on DMs and bookings
- Rule: none of the day job's customers

**Question script** (5-10 minutes, in person, on the phone, or by message):
1. "What's the most annoying repetitive thing you do every week with messages, bookings, or follow-ups?"
2. "How many hours a week does that eat?"
3. "What have you tried to fix it?" (Listen for: tried AI or tools and gave up = good sign)
4. "If that just handled itself, what would it be worth to you?"
5. "Anyone else you know who deals with the same thing?"

**Write down for each:** business type, the problem, hours per week, what they've tried, what it's worth to them.

**Pass/fail:** If 3 or more name the same kind of problem and put a real dollar value on it, move on.
If they shrug, we change the customer type or the offer before you build anything.

## Weeks 2-4: First builds and first leads

- **Week 2:** Offer 1-2 builds at a discount in exchange for a testimonial and an honest answer to "would you have paid full price?" Build on weekends.
- **Week 2:** Set up an Upwork profile focused on no-code AI automation. Send 2-3 proposals a week on weeknights.
- **Week 3:** List 10 possible partners (web designers, marketing agencies, bookkeepers, property managers). Ryker approves the list and any message before it goes out.
- **Week 3-4:** Post 2-3 short videos a week showing the discounted builds (with the owner's permission). Screen recording plus a quick before and after.
- **Week 4:** Pitch one local workshop, "AI for small business owners," to the Chamber of Commerce or a local meetup.

## Path to quitting (rough, not a promise)

- Full speed: 2 builds/mo at about $2,000 plus 10 care plans at $200 is about $6,000/mo in a good month.
- Quit point: care plans plus about 1 build/mo reliably cover $4-5K. Expect several months, not weeks.
- After quitting: hire a helper for care plans and grow into a small agency.
